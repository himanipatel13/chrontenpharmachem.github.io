Pharmaceutical Intermediates Manufacturer India

Chronten Pharmachem is a trusted pharmaceutical intermediates manufacturer in Gujarat, India, supplying high-purity API intermediates and key starting materials (KSMs) to generic drug manufacturers worldwide. Our product portfolio covers intermediates for leading therapies: Pregabalin intermediate (CAS 181289-33-8) for neuropathic pain, Clopidogrel bisulphate intermediate (CAS 141109-19-5) as an antiplatelet agent, Pantoprazole sodium intermediate (CAS 72830-09-2) for acid reflux treatment, Dapagliflozin intermediates for type 2 diabetes, Lercanidipine and Benidipine intermediates for hypertension, plus Fexofenadine, Amisulpride, Levosulpiride and Azelnidipine intermediates.

Buyers searching by CAS number or looking for a reliable KSM manufacturer, custom synthesis partner or bulk drug intermediate exporter from India can request quotations, COA, MSDS and regulatory documentation directly. Located in the Gujarat chemical belt serving Ankleshwar, Panoli and Vapi industrial estates, we ship globally with full batch traceability and GMP-aligned quality systems.
